Pocket Booklet Maker

Turns a PDF of small booklet pages into a print-ready sheet with four pages on each side of letter paper, arranged so they come out in order once you cut, fold, and staple. Your file stays on your computer.

Printing and assembly

  1. Print double-sided, flip on long edge, at actual size.
  2. Trim the left and right edges at the marks, then cut each sheet in half across the middle.
  3. Stack the half-sheets so the one showing the last page and page 1 is on top. The table below shows the order for your file.
  4. Fold the stack so page 1 is on the front, and staple on the fold.

Pages can be up to 4.25 x 5.5 inches. If your page count isn't a multiple of 8, blank pages are added just before the back cover.
